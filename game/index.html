SOLMAR CITY

AN OPEN-WORLD CRIME STORY

You are Mara Voss, a courier pulled into the Solmar underworld. Explore the sun-soaked coastal city on foot or behind the wheel — rain and storms roll in off the sea and make the roads slick, so watch the sky. Steal a car, cruise the beachfront, dodge the law, and run twelve jobs for Rico — the streets, the traffic and the police are all live. Buy a pistol at the gun shop, work the night shift, guard a client, drive a taxi, run contraband past the roadblocks, outrun a three-star chase, and photograph the city. Sail a boat across the bay to the second island, light its lighthouse after dark, and take turf from the Vipers, Sharks and Crows — every turf you hold pays $50 a minute. Tune your ride at the Solmar Garage. Fifteen hidden packages are stashed around town — find them all for a $1,000 bonus. And when Rico has no work, the city still pays: fish off the east pier, race the avenues from the garage flag, run courier parcels, try the Golden Gull casino, buy a safehouse to wake up in, and keep Biscuit the stray dog fed.
